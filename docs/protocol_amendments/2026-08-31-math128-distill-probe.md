# External probe test on public R1-Distill-7B samples (math128 dump)
(2026-08-31, frozen after sample verification, before any state extraction)

## Purpose

Run the difficulty-controlled early-window probe test in the strong
proponents' regime (R1-Distill family, competition math) without any
generation, using `nishadsinghi/math128_solutions_r1_distill_qwen_7b_32K_tokens`:
128 MATH problems × 256 sampled solutions from DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B
(32K budget; generation prompt stored verbatim). The 256-samples-per-problem
design permits the control no published probe positive can run: a
**within-problem** evaluation — given the problem, does the early window of
THIS attempt predict THIS attempt's outcome beyond the problem's base rate?

## Data status (already computed, CPU only)

All 32,768 samples verified with our MATH verifier against the boxed
reference: overall pass rate 0.929; 64 problems saturated (256/256), 42 in
[0.95, 1.0), and a 22-problem mid-band (rate 0.055–0.945) holding 2,178 of
the 2,323 failures. Verification table:
`artifacts/external/math128_distill7b/sample_correctness.parquet`.

## Frozen extraction plan

- Model for re-scoring: `mlx-community/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B-4bit`
  (teacher-forcing the dump's own tokens; no sampling anywhere).
- Trajectory set: (a) **within-problem set** — all 256 samples of the 22
  mid-band problems (5,632); (b) **pooled set** — 8 samples per problem for
  all 128 problems, frozen seed 20260831 (mid-band rows reused from (a)).
  Total 6,480 forward passes.
- Window: prompt + first 512 generated-text tokens (re-tokenized with the
  model's tokenizer; retokenization drift recorded).
- Stored per trajectory (no full state dumps): mean-pooled final-layer
  hidden state over the last 4 reasoning tokens at each probe anchor
  t ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 192, 256, 384, 512} (David's grid); token
  scalar streams (entropy, surprisal, top-1 probability) over the window;
  **fidelity metric**: share of dump tokens that are the 4-bit model's
  top-1 prediction (quantization/precision mismatch is the known caveat —
  the dump was generated by a full-precision server).

## Frozen analysis plan

- Probes: (i) replication-style raw-state probe (PCA ≤ 128 + L2 logistic)
  per anchor t; (ii) our frozen 15-block summary features over the window.
- Training always on problem-disjoint splits (StratifiedGroupKFold over
  problems, 5 folds, both sets pooled for training).
- Endpoints per anchor t:
  1. **Pooled AUROC** on held-out problems (what the literature reports).
  2. **Within-problem AUROC** on held-out mid-band problems: per-problem
     AUROC over that problem's 256 samples, failure-count-weighted mean,
     with a problem-clustered bootstrap CI.
  3. Reference rows: the LOO pass-rate baseline (by construction: pooled
     high, within-problem exactly 0.5) and chance.
- Reading, recorded in advance: pooled ≫ within-problem indicates the
  pooled positives are mostly difficulty; within-problem materially above
  0.5 (CI excluding 0.5) is a genuine within-attempt early signal and is
  reported as such — it would qualify our C6 as feature- or regime-limited,
  not overturn the control's necessity. Either outcome is informative and
  final; no retuning after unblinding.

## Caveats (recorded now)

4-bit re-scoring of full-precision generations (fidelity metric reported);
retokenization of decoded text; dump's sampling temperature not documented;
MATH-train origin of problems is irrelevant here (no benchmark claim);
mid-band selection conditions on pass rate only (an X-side variable), never
on any probe outcome.

## Outcome (recorded 2026-08-31, single run of the frozen battery)

Extraction: 6,480/6,480 forwards; mean top-1 fidelity 0.906 (p10 0.859).
Results (state probe; problem-clustered 95% CIs):

- t = 4: pooled 0.849 [0.735, 0.919] — the uncontrolled original's 0.84
  replicates in this regime; within-problem 0.496 [0.466, 0.527] — chance.
- Within-problem is at chance at every anchor (all CIs contain 0.5;
  22 problems, failure weight 2,178).
- Pooled decays to ~0.55 by t = 16–32 and stays there: the difficulty
  content readable from last-token states is a prompt echo that fades as
  generation proceeds (composition held fixed, so this is state decay, not
  the original's sample-attrition story).
- LOO pass-rate reference: pooled 0.974. Its within-problem AUROC is
  exactly 0 — the anticipated mechanical artifact in extreme form (within a
  problem LOO takes two values and the incorrect row's is always higher);
  the interpretive reference for "no within-attempt information" remains
  0.5, which the probes sit on.
- Stream-summary probe: pooled 0.647 at t = 4, within 0.490 — same story.
- Numerics: float32 results reproduced in float64 (0 NaN out-of-fold
  rows); sklearn overflow warnings traced to near-constant scaled
  dimensions and are cosmetic.

Reading per the frozen plan: the pooled positive is entirely
problem-identity; no within-attempt early signal exists in this regime
either. Reconciliations: consistent with Lugoloobi et al.'s
declining-signal-with-budget and with probes at special answer positions
(Zhang et al.) rather than arbitrary early positions.

## Audit re-analysis (2026-09-01, external critique applied)

Two implementation corrections from the pre-submission audit, both

- The LOO baseline now uses the full 32,768-sample verification table

  0.496 [0.466, 0.527]; chance under both weightings at all ten anchors.
Outputs: analysis_v2/. Conclusions unchanged.

## Post-hoc within-problem robustness check (2026-09-02, after reporting; not pre-registered)

Motivation: a pooled-trained probe may fail to carry within-attempt
information simply because problem identity dominates its training signal.
Two checks on the stored states of the 22-problem within set
(`scripts/analyze_math128_within_robustness.py`, `..._within_followup.py`;
outputs in `analysis_v2/within_robustness_*.json`):

- **A. Problem-centered transfer probe** (per-problem mean removed from the
  states, label-free; problem-disjoint 5-fold; PCA<=128 + balanced logistic):
  failure-weighted within AUROC 0.490 [0.464, 0.523] at t=4 (chance), rising
  to 0.556 [0.522, 0.588] at t=32 and 0.52–0.54 at t>=64 (several CIs exclude
  0.5; within-problem label-permutation null spans 0.47–0.53).
- **B. Per-problem oracle** (sample-disjoint 5-fold inside each problem,
  PCA 32): failure-weighted mean 0.476 at t=4 (chance; permutation null
  0.44–0.49), 0.558 at t=64 (null 0.44–0.50). Heterogeneous: 3 of 22
  problems (ids 20, 22, 13; 84 of 2,178 failures) are strongly separable at
  every anchor including t=4 (AUROC 0.92 / 0.84 / 0.78; permutation null
  maximum <= 0.61), with per-problem AUROCs consistent across anchors
  (Spearman 0.71 between t=32 and t=64). In problems 20 and 22 the failures
  are 4–6x shorter than the successes (an early answer-without-reasoning
  mode); in 13 lengths and fidelity match (a genuine early content
  divergence). High-failure problems (e.g. 83, 81) show no separability.
- **C. Uncentered re-run** on the within set alone reproduces the frozen
  battery qualitatively (0.515 [0.488, 0.547] at t=4; the battery trains on
  both sets and reports 0.496 [0.466, 0.527]).

Reading: the frozen conclusion stands (the reconstructed, pooled-trained
probe carries no within-attempt information at any anchor, and the
published-style early-window positive is between-problem). Within-attempt
information does exist, but it is small on average, appears from t~32
rather than in the earliest window, and is concentrated in a few
low-failure problems — the rare failures on easy problems are legible
early; the common failures on hard problems are not. "Possibly entirely
between-problem" is therefore too strong; "predominantly" is the accurate
scope.
